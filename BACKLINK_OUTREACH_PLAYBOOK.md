# Backlink Outreach Playbook

Site: `https://biblequizcompetition.com`

## Goal
Earn relevant, high-trust backlinks from:
- Church websites
- Youth ministry blogs
- Christian education resources
- Sunday school directories
- Homeschool curriculum/resource pages

## Pages To Promote First
1. `/public-quiz`
2. `/bible-questions`
3. `/prayers`
4. `/free-bible-quiz-questions-and-answers`
5. `/bible-trivia-questions-and-answers`
6. `/media-kit`

## Weekly Execution (Repeat)
1. Find 20 prospects
- Use search terms:
  - `intitle:resources "youth ministry" "bible quiz"`
  - `"Sunday school resources" "Bible questions"`
  - `"Christian homeschool" "Bible trivia"`
  - `"church activities" "Bible quiz"`

2. Qualify quickly
- Relevant audience (church/youth/education)
- Has outbound resource links
- Not spammy

3. Send 10 personalized emails
- Reference one specific page/post from their site.
- Offer one matching resource page from us.
- Keep email under 120 words.

4. Follow up after 5-7 days
- Single short follow-up.
- No more than 2 follow-ups.

5. Track responses and wins
- Record every contact in a sheet (date, site, status, URL).

## Email Template (Short)
Subject: Free Bible quiz resource for your [audience]

Hi [Name],  
I enjoyed your [specific page/post] and thought this might help your [students/youth group/readers].  
We built a free Bible quiz resource with book-wise and chapter-wise practice:  
https://biblequizcompetition.com/public-quiz  

If useful, feel free to include it in your resources page.  
Also sharing our media kit for official wording:  
https://biblequizcompetition.com/media-kit  

Thanks for your ministry/work,  
[Your Name]

## Anchor Text Mix (Use Naturally)
- Bible quiz questions and answers
- free Bible quiz
- chapter-wise Bible quiz
- Bible questions and answers
- Bible trivia questions

## What To Avoid
- Buying links
- Exact-match anchor spam
- Unrelated directory submissions
- Mass identical outreach

## Monthly Targets
- 40-60 quality outreaches
- 6-12 positive responses
- 3-8 live backlinks from relevant domains
