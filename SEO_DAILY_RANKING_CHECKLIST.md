# SEO Daily Execution System (90 Days)

Site: `https://biblequizcompetition.com`  
Start date: `2026-05-12`  
Primary goal: outrank current competitors for quiz, lyrics, and verse keyword clusters through better content quality, structure, and internal linking.

## How We Use This File Daily
- Open this file first.
- Complete the `Daily Must-Do (90-minute block)` section.
- Execute the current sprint week tasks.
- Append one `Daily Log` entry at end of day.

## Daily Must-Do (90-minute block)
1. Pull Search Console performance for last 7 days vs previous 7 days.
2. Pick 1 primary page to improve today (from sprint plan below).
3. Add/upgrade:
- 1 direct answer intro block
- 3-5 FAQ Q&As matching real queries
- 8-12 internal links (in + out)
4. Improve title + meta for 1 page with low CTR.
5. Request indexing for updated page.
6. Log outcomes in `Daily Log Template`.

## 90-Day Sprint Plan

### Sprint 1 (Weeks 1-2): Foundation + Cannibalization Fix
Goal: make all primary intent pages clearly mapped and stronger than current versions.

Week 1
1. Final keyword-to-URL map freeze (one keyword intent per URL).
2. Rewrite intros/H1s for top pages:
- `/bible-quiz-questions-and-answers`
- `/bible-quiz-for-sunday-school`
- `/free-bible-quiz-no-signup`
- `/old-testament-quiz`
- `/new-testament-quiz`
3. Add FAQ schema blocks to all above pages.
4. Add canonical consistency check (`non-www`) and sitemap refresh.

Week 2
1. Improve lyrics money pages:
- `/hindi-songs`
- `/hindi-christian-songs-lyrics-chords`
- `/ho-teri-stuti-aur-aradhana-lyrics-telugu-kannada-malayalam`
- `/apna-bojh-prabhu-par-daal-lyrics-chords`
- `/hallelujah-stuti-gaye-hum-lyrics`
2. Ensure each has: full lyrics, chords/meaning section, FAQ, related links.
3. Add 8+ internal links into each page from related hubs.

### Sprint 2 (Weeks 3-4): Build Supporting Clusters
Goal: publish support pages that push authority to head pages.

Week 3 (Quiz Support Cluster)
1. Publish 10 support pages targeting long-tail quiz intents:
- easy bible quiz questions and answers
- bible quiz questions for youth fellowship
- sunday school bible quiz printable
- bible true or false quiz
- etc.
2. Every support page links to 2 money pages + 3 sibling pages.

Week 4 (Lyrics Support Cluster)
1. Publish 10 support pages targeting:
- `<song> lyrics in hindi`
- `<song> chords`
- `<song> meaning`
2. Add item list style “related songs by intent” blocks.

### Sprint 3 (Weeks 5-6): Verses Cluster Upgrade
Goal: move verse pages toward top 10/top 15 for head terms.

Week 5
1. Upgrade:
- `/verses/strength`
- `/verses/love`
- `/verses/hope`
- `/verses/faith`
2. Add contextual sections:
- when to pray these verses
- short reflection for each verse group
- related verse links

Week 6
1. Publish 8 supporting verse pages (anxiety, fear, healing, peace, etc.).
2. Create strong cross-link ring among verse pages.

### Sprint 4 (Weeks 7-8): CTR + Snippet Win Sprint
Goal: improve click-through without waiting for new pages.

Week 7
1. Find top 20 high-impression, low-CTR queries.
2. Update title/meta on 10 pages (1 change per page only).
3. Add “last updated” date and trust blocks (author/reviewer).

Week 8
1. Review CTR impact from week 7 changes.
2. Keep winners, revert weak variants.
3. Add new FAQ questions from query data.

### Sprint 5 (Weeks 9-10): Depth + Trust Signals
Goal: outrank by quality and completeness.

Week 9
1. Expand top 10 pages to full depth:
- concise answer first
- TOC
- practical examples
- FAQ
- internal next steps
2. Add editorial policy and “how content is maintained” references.

Week 10
1. Add source references and scriptural context where relevant.
2. Improve structured data coverage (FAQ, Breadcrumb, ItemList, Video).

### Sprint 6 (Weeks 11-12): Consolidate Winners + Prune Losers
Goal: focus crawl budget and authority on pages that can rank.

Week 11
1. Identify underperforming pages with overlap intent.
2. Merge or retarget thin/duplicate pages.
3. Strengthen internal links into pages sitting position 8-20.

Week 12
1. Final 90-day performance audit:
- query winners
- CTR winners
- pages entering top 10/top 20
2. Set next 90-day plan by cluster.

## Daily Priority Queue (Always Work Top-Down)
1. Pages ranking in positions 8-20 for high-intent terms.
2. Pages with high impressions but CTR below 2.5%.
3. Money pages missing complete sections (FAQ/chords/meaning/examples).
4. Support pages that can pass internal authority.

## Content Quality Checklist (before publishing/updating)
- Exact primary keyword in title, H1, first paragraph.
- Search intent matched in first 100 words.
- No fluff sections.
- FAQ includes real query phrasing.
- 8+ internal links and 2+ outbound useful references if appropriate.
- Schema valid and canonical correct.
- Mobile readability checked.

## KPI Targets (90 Days)
1. Top 10 for at least 5 quiz head terms.
2. Top 20 for at least 20 Hindi lyrics/chords terms.
3. Top 15 for 4 verse head terms.
4. +40% organic clicks overall vs baseline.
5. +25% CTR on pages updated during Sprint 4.
